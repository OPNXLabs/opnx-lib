# Contributing

[Korean](CONTRIBUTING.ko.md)

OPNX.Lib is currently published as source-available preview software for evaluation and technical review.

Issue reports and technical feedback are welcome. Before reporting a security vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and do not disclose the vulnerability in a public issue.

External pull requests are not accepted until OPNX establishes a contribution licensing policy. Do not submit code changes unless an OPNX maintainer has requested them and confirmed the applicable contribution terms in writing.

Submitting an issue, suggestion, or other feedback does not transfer ownership of your pre-existing intellectual property to OPNX. Do not include confidential information, customer data, credentials, or material you do not have the right to share.

Commercial licensing and partnership inquiries: `opnx@opnx.kr`
