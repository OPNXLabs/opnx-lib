FFmpeg itself is a separate third-party project with its own license terms.
Depending on the build configuration and enabled components, FFmpeg may be
available under LGPL or GPL terms.

native binaries under the OPNX source-available license.

Users who obtain, bundle, or redistribute FFmpeg native binaries must review
the license terms for the specific FFmpeg build they use.
